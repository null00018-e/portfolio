Reflection

01

What was your greatest accomplishment this nine weeks?

My biggest accomplishment is making my e-portfolio. I had fun designing it, especially the home page. It took some time to make, and the quality of the site makes up for the work it took to make it. I do intend on updating the site more in the future and improving the current pages. Documenting my robotics work and doing more research on it while making the portfolio has helped me significantly to understand in more depth what I didn't even realize I didn't know about building and coding the robot.

02

What was your biggest challenge?

My greatest challenge was coding the robot for the Speedy Delivery activity. It was difficult to do because I had trouble calculating the measurements for the arm and claw. Also, if you messed up your measurements, you had to retry it because the robot over-rotated and just stopped working, which was hard to deal with because the line was long usually to re-run your code. On top of all that, the robot motors and weights were slightly imbalanced, so it would veer slightly to one side and mess up the whole run. Despite this, I was able to pick up 2/3 “packages” and deliver them. Though this was difficult, it helped me understand the robot and code much more than I previously did.
